# My SwiftUI Final Project
## Background/Overview
I am a huge fan of Magic: The Gathering. I once asked my friend when we could play the game next, and he said that he didn't really want to play because he didn't like his decks and wanted to find something new to play with. Enter: The Randomander. This swiftUI app uses ScryFall's free API to grab 5 random commanders from their random commander endpoint. This solves my friend's problem of not knowing which decck to play next, just open the app and it'll choose for you! Don't like that option? Head over to the selection screen and pick another random one of 5. Don't like those options? Close the app and re-open it to get 5 more options. This project was built in ~12 hours of work time with many concepts being learned and re-inforced along the way.

## Improvements
This was a final project for my SwiftUI mobile app course. It's not perfect my any means. It needs a button that will automatically refresh the commander options, better UI design, removal of unnecessary NavigationTabs and solving for edge-cases, but overall I think this is a great example of what I'm capable of doing in a short amount of time.
